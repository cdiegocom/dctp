## Key calibration after the R1 revision

The contribution of the article is the **operational coupling** of four
otherwise-parallel governance practices (integrity, fairness, synthesis,
provenance), not the empirical demonstration that naive synthesis can
worsen fairness — that empirical observation is well-established in the
cited literature and is treated as corroborative rather than novel.

The four synthesis protocols are not interchangeable in role:

| Protocol | Role             | Empirical work in this evaluation                |
| -------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| P1       | Discriminator    | Variable pass rate across generators             |
| P2       | Discriminator    | Fires rollback in 20/20 multi-seed configs       |
| P3       | Record invariant | 100% pass — guards against pathologies not run   |
| P4       | Record invariant | 100% pass — guards against pathologies not run   |

The three conflict-resolution protocols are also distinguished:

| Protocol             | Status in this evaluation                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Fairness–Synthesis  | Exercised (fires across all multi-seed configs) |
| Integrity–Fairness  | Specified; not triggered (uniform exclusions)   |
| Provenance–Integrity| Specified; not triggered (no schema migration)  |

## Total empirical scope

- 32 dataset-generator-seed configurations covering 26 independent
  pipeline executions: 20 multi-seed (Gaussian Copula at 10 seeds × 2
  datasets) + 12 multi-generator (CTGAN and TVAE at 3 seeds × 2
  datasets, with the 6 seed-overlapping Gaussian Copula runs reused
  from the multi-seed sweep counted once).

  decision coverage 1.0 in every configuration.

  lines of experiment scripts.
